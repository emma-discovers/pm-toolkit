# PRD Template

Lightweight, scannable, built for engineers and designers.

## Structure

1. **Context & Goals** — why now, what we're solving.
2. **User Problem** — who hurts, how.
3. **Scope** — in / out.
4. **Success Metrics** — how we measure.
5. **Risks & Dependencies** — what could go wrong.
6. **Open Questions** — what we still don't know.

## Example (anonymized)

### Context & Goals

A corporate learning platform with 14,000+ IT specialists.  
Adoption of existing tools was low, NPS at 32.  
One of the goals: increase engagement and satisfaction.

### User Problem

Specialists can't see how learning connects to their career.  
The catalog is generic, navigation is unclear.

### Scope

**In:** personalized development tracks, onboarding flow, progress dashboard.  
**Out:** content production, LMS migration, mobile app.

### Success Metrics

- Adoption: 25% within 4 months.
- NPS: 32 → 65.
- Time-to-fill for IT roles: −15%.

### Risks & Dependencies

- Dependency: integration with HR data (grade, role, performance).
- Risk: personalization without enough data → generic tracks.

### Open Questions

- How do we measure long-term behavior change, not just completion?
- What's the minimum data needed for personalization to feel real?

---

**Tip:** I prefer to keep it to one page. In regulated or complex contexts, it can be longer, but the core is always the same: problem, scope, metrics, risks.
